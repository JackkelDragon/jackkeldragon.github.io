Blackstar Squad Prequel Stories

Published: Tue 06 October 2026
By Jackkel Dragon

In misc.

tags: blackstar books web stories

As Blackstar Squad is prepared for its full release, I'm posting a few short stories to the website that depict the events leading up to the main story. (These stories will also be included in the full version of the book, but will likely be placed at the end of the book to maintain the intended pacing of the story.)

Check out the Blackstar Squad prequel stories here.

Some additional context for the prequel stories:

  • Relocation takes place a few years before the opening of the main story, focusing on the current social environment of the Argent Republic.
  • The other three stories take place a few weeks before the main story begins, introducing some of the main characters as they were before the story.
