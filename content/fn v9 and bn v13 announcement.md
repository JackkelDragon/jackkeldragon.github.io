Title: Updates to Blooming Nightshade Games
Date: 2026-11-18
Tags: BN, FN, RN, nightshade, updates

[Blooming Nightshade]({filename}/pages/nightshade/bloomingnightshade.md) and [Flowering Nightshade]({filename}/pages/nightshade/floweringnightshade.md), the lighthearted romance visual novels about Himeka and her friends, have recieved new updates. BN is now at v13, while FN is at v9.

Shared Changes:

- Story selection screen updated
- Save names now use story titles
- Some UI tweaks
- Updated Ren'Py to 8.6.1

Flowering Nightshade:

- Added the story "Belated Love"
- Numerous tweaks to Sim Akiramine (see [prior devlog]({filename}/fn sim akiramine plans for v9.md) for some details)
- (Steam only) Achievement sets for v8 and v9 stories

Steam users may continue to use an older build via the betas tab (as usual), but I may also leave FN v8 public on itch.io due to the significant changes to Sim Akiramine.

With the release of "Belated Love", all of the stories that were planned and outlined for Flowering Nightshade have been finished. At the current time, there are no further plans to add stories to either Blooming Nightshade or Flowering Nightshade. (If there are bugs, please report them and I'll release patches or hotfixes.)

A potential project continuing the story of characters from Blooming Nightshade is under consideration, and I may seek feedback on possible next steps in the near future. For now, here's a reminder of the previous teaser image:
